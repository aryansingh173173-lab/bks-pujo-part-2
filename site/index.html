Cultural context first

The Puja & Integrated Farming

You are invited to the worship, music and food, and to the farm growing behind the pandal.

The days of the Puja

What each day of the Puja means.

  • Mahalaya

    Pitru Paksha ends. In Bengal this dawn is also cultural memory: the goddess is turned toward the living year.

  • Shashthi

    Bodhon: the public awakening of the image.

  • Saptami

    Nabapatrika / Kolabou. Full public worship typically begins.

  • Ashtami

    Pushpanjali, Kumari Puja, and Sandhi Puja at the join of Ashtami and Navami.

  • Navami

    Culminating worship in many committees.

  • Dashami

    Sindoor Khela, Bijoya, and immersion, often on a civil day also fixed with the police.

At a glance

Who, what, when, where

Who
Bharatiya Krishak Samaj, West Bengal
What
A seasonal Durga Puja that names the farmer, shows Integrated Farming, and invites Non-Resident Bengalis to seed village farms.
When
Durga Puja 2026. Civic holidays are documented. Ritual clocks await a named Panjika.
Where
Munshi Bheri, Mahish Bathan, Salt Lake Sector 5, Kolkata 700102 (near Panache residential complex)
Why
The Puja is when Bengal gathers. This gathering puts the annadata in view, then shows a farm livelihood that patrons can help seed.
What next
